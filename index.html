Everything you need, nothing you don't

QuickTemp puts all the essentials on one screen — no tapping through menus.

🌡️

Live Temperature

Current temperature with feels-like, humidity, wind speed and weather description — updated automatically.

🧭

Accurate Compass

Real magnetic compass using your device sensor. Always points north — works offline.

⛰️

Altitude & Location

Precise GPS altitude updated every 10 seconds. Tap to open an interactive map of your position.

🌊

Tide Schedule

Daily high and low tide times with a visual chart. Essential for coastal activities.

🌙

Sun & Night Hours

Countdown to sunrise or sunset based on your actual location. Day/night detected automatically.

💨

Air Quality & UV

European AQI index and UV radiation level so you know when to take precautions outdoors.

🌧️

Rain Radar

Live precipitation radar so you can see rain approaching before it arrives.

📅

Hourly Forecast

Hour-by-hour precipitation and wind forecast with a clean visual chart.

⭐

Favourite Cities

Save multiple locations and switch between them instantly. Check weather anywhere in the world.
